# PACE Eligibility and Enrollment

## Who qualifies

To enroll in PACE, a person generally must meet all of the following criteria:

- Be 55 years of age or older.
- Live in the service area of a PACE organization.
- Be certified by the state as needing a nursing home level of care.
- Be able to live safely in the community with the help of PACE services at the
  time of enrollment.

Meeting the nursing-home level-of-care standard does not mean the participant must
live in a nursing home. The goal of PACE is precisely to serve people who would
otherwise qualify for institutional care but who can remain safely at home with
comprehensive support.

## Dual eligibility and costs

Many PACE participants are dually eligible for Medicare and Medicaid. What a
participant pays depends on their financial situation:

- If a participant qualifies for both Medicare and Medicaid, they typically pay
  nothing for the long-term care portion of the PACE benefit.
- If a participant has Medicare but not Medicaid, they generally pay a monthly
  premium for the long-term care portion plus a premium for Medicare Part D drugs.
- There is no deductible or copayment for any drug, service, or care approved by
  the PACE interdisciplinary team.

## Enrollment process

Enrollment is voluntary. A prospective participant (or a family member or referrer)
contacts a local PACE organization, which then conducts an intake and a
comprehensive assessment by the interdisciplinary team. The state determines
whether the applicant meets the nursing-home level-of-care requirement.

Once enrolled, participants generally must receive all care through the PACE
organization and its network. This is an important counseling point during
enrollment: except for emergency care, services from providers outside the PACE
network are typically not covered.

## Voluntary disenrollment

Participation is voluntary, and participants may disenroll at any time. Upon
disenrollment, the organization assists with an orderly transition back to other
Medicare and Medicaid coverage options.
